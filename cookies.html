1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, work more efficiently, and to provide information to the site owner.

2. How we use cookies

This Site uses a limited set of cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

CategoryPurpose
EssentialRequired for core site functionality, such as remembering your navigation state.
AnalyticsHelp us understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve it (used only if analytics is enabled — see the GA4 / Meta Pixel placeholders in the site source).
PreferenceRemember choices you make, such as a completed inquiry form state, to improve your experience on return visits.

3. Third-party cookies

Where analytics tools such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel are enabled on this Site, those providers may set their own cookies subject to their respective privacy and cookie policies.

4. Managing cookies

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting them. Restricting cookies may affect the functionality of this Site. You can find out more about managing cookies at aboutcookies.org.
