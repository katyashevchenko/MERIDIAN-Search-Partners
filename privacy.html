Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Meridian Search Partners ("Meridian," "we," "us") respects the privacy of everyone who visits this site or engages with our executive search services. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Contact details submitted through our inquiry form (name, email, company, role hiring for, company stage, mandate context).
  • Correspondence you send us by email or telephone.
  • Candidate information shared with us in the course of a retained search, where you have consented to that sharing.

We also collect limited technical information automatically, such as browser type, device type, and general usage analytics, to help us understand how the site is used and to improve it.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to inquiries submitted through this site and to discuss a potential search mandate.
  • To evaluate and present candidates to clients as part of a retained search, with appropriate consent.
  • To maintain our records of client and candidate relationships.
  • To improve the content, functionality, and security of this site.

3. Legal basis for processing

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on: your consent (for example, when you submit the inquiry form), the necessity of processing to take steps toward a contract (for example, discussing a search mandate), and our legitimate interests in operating and improving our business, balanced against your rights.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell personal data. We may share information with:

  • Clients, where you are a candidate who has consented to be presented for a specific mandate.
  • Service providers who support our operations (for example, hosting, email, and analytics providers), under confidentiality obligations.
  • Regulators or authorities where required by law.

5. Data retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, including maintaining candidate and client relationships over time, and to comply with legal and regulatory obligations. You may request deletion of your data at any time, subject to any legal retention requirements.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to certain processing or request a copy of your data in a portable format. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International transfers

As a firm operating across London and New York, personal data may be processed or stored outside your country of residence. Where this occurs, we take steps to ensure an appropriate level of protection consistent with applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be reflected here.
